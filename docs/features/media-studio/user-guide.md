# Godance Media Studio & Community Gallery - User Guide

Welcome to the Godance Media Studio! This guide walks you through creating high-fidelity images and videos, discovering curated recipes, enhancing outputs with post-processing tools, and earning credit rewards by sharing your work with the community.

---

## 1. Creating Media Assets in the Studio

Access the studio via the top navigation bar at **Studio** (or navigate to `/generate`).

### 1.1 Selecting a Modality and Model Engine
- **Image Generation**:
  - **FLUX 1.1 Pro**: Exceptional photographic realism, lighting nuance, and texture fidelity. Ideal for character portraits, concept art, and realistic environments.
  - **DALL-E 3**: Superior prompt adherence. Best when scenes contain complex multi-object compositions or exact prompt descriptions.
  - **Recraft v3**: Specialized in vector graphics, icons, and UI design assets.
- **Video Generation**:
  - **Runway Gen-3 Alpha**: Cinematic motion blur, camera tracking, and atmospheric lighting.
  - **Kling 1.5**: Robust character motion and physics fidelity. Supports Standard and Professional rendering qualities.
  - **Luma Dream Machine**: Fluid natural motion and expansive camera sweeps.

### 1.2 Fine-Tuning Parameters
- **Aspect Ratio**: Select your desired framing (`1:1 Square`, `16:9 Landscape`, `9:16 Portrait`, `4:3 Standard`).
- **Duration & Quality** (Video Mode): Pick output duration (`5s` or `10s`) and quality profiles (`720p HD`, `1080p Full HD`).
- **Expert Settings**:
  - **Negative Prompt**: Describe elements to exclude (e.g. `blurry, deformed, oversaturated`).
  - **Seed**: Enter a numeric seed for reproducible generation, or tap the **🎲 Dice** button to randomize.
  - **Reference Image**: Attach a base image in video mode to steer motion synthesis from a starting frame (Image-to-Video).

---

## 2. Using Curated Prompt Recipes

When starting from a blank prompt, explore verified recipes:

1. Click **✨ Browse Recipes** above the prompt textarea.
2. In the inspiration modal:
   - Search by keyword (e.g. `pixel`, `drone`, `cyberpunk`, `sumi-e`).
   - Filter by topic (`🎮 Game Assets`, `📸 Photography`, `🎬 Motion Video`, `🎨 Art`).
3. Click any visual card to immediately hydrate the prompt and automatically configure the optimal model, aspect ratio, and video duration.
4. A **`🌱 Forked from: [Recipe Name]`** lineage badge will attach to your prompt box, preserving creative credit.

---

## 3. Post-Processing Value-Add Tools

Once generation completes, access the tool actions below the canvas:

- **✂️ Remove Background (Cutout)**:
  Extracts the primary subject with an alpha transparency channel, displaying the asset over a checkerboard grid. Ideal for game sprites and UI elements.
- **🔍 Upscale to 4K (Ultra Detail)**:
  Increases output resolution up to 4K with enhanced sharpness and reduced compression artifacts.

---

## 4. Community Gallery & Share-to-Earn Rewards

### 4.1 Discover & Remix Creations
- Navigate to **Gallery** (or `/gallery`):
  - Explore outputs from official curators and fellow creators.
  - Hover over video cards to trigger silent loop playback.
  - Tap any card to open the inspection Lightbox, copy full prompt text, and review generation seeds.
  - Click **`⚡ Remix in Studio`** to load the exact parameters into the Studio workspace.

### 4.2 Sharing Outputs to Earn Credit Rewards
- Below your generation canvas, tap **🚀 Publish & Share (+10¢)**.
- Confirm publication to the public gallery.
- Each successful public share automatically adds **$0.10 (10 Cents)** to your credit balance.
- **Daily Quota**: Up to **5 reward claims per day** ($0.50 daily credit bonus). Progress is tracked via the daily badge in the top navigation.
